Essentials

Practical Travel Information

Gear & Packing Tips

  • Hiking Boots: Sturdy, broken-in trail boots with tread are required for Day 2 (Stoos) and Day 7 (Oeschinensee).
  • Mountain Layers: Fleece, windbreakers, and light rain jackets are essential. Summit elevations exceed 3,800m.
  • Milan Dress Code: Knees and shoulders must be covered to enter Milan Duomo and Santa Maria delle Grazie.

Transit & Logistics

  • Swiss Transit (SBB App): Keep the SBB mobile app installed. It acts as your live timetable and pass wallet.
  • Currency & Coins: CHF in Switzerland, EUR in Italy. Have CHF coins for Lauterbrunnen luggage lockers.
  • Car-Free Zones: Wengen and Zermatt are car-free. All travel within these villages is walking or electric taxi.
