### 🥾 3. Packing & Gear Checklist

*   [ ] **High-Quality Hiking Footwear:** Broken-in hiking boots or trail shoes with substantial grip are **essential**. Avoid casual sneakers for the Stoos Ridge (Day 2), Heuberg Loop (Day 7), and Gornergrat-to-Riffelberg (Day 9) trails.
*   [ ] **High-Altitude Outerwear:** Mountain weather is highly unpredictable. Pack a windbreaker, light rain jacket, and warm layers (fleece or packable down) for:
    *   Glacier/mountain summits: Matterhorn Glacier Paradise (Day 10 reaches 3,883m), Gornergrat (Day 9 reaches 3,089m), and Grindelwald-First (Day 6).
    *   Moist/cold interiors: Trümmelbach Falls (Day 4 is cold and misty).
*   [ ] **Religious Site Dress Code Clothing:** Prepare outfits that cover **shoulders and knees** for church entrances in Milan, specifically the Duomo and Santa Maria delle Grazie. (Detail in [DAY_12.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_12.md))
*   [ ] **Cash and Coins (CHF & EUR):**
    *   Keep Swiss Franc (CHF) coins/cards handy for renting luggage lockers at Lauterbrunnen station. (Detail in [DAY_04.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_04.md))
    *   Carry some Swiss Franc cash for small mountain farm purchases (e.g., cheese at Unterbärgli/Oberbärgli on Day 7).
*   [ ] **Sun Protection:** High-altitude UV rays are strong. Pack sunscreen, polarized sunglasses, and a wide-brimmed hat.
*   [ ] **Valid Passports:** Passports must be carried during the Swiss-Italian border crossing on the train. (Detail in [DAY_10.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_10.md))

## 🗺️ Day-by-Day On-Site Ticketing Action Items

Follow this chronological checklist during your trip to purchase local tickets and handle transit logistics.

*   [ ] **Day 1 (Sept 8): Arrival**
    *   If not purchased online, buy your Swiss Travel Pass (STP) at the Zurich Airport (ZRH) SBB travel center before boarding the train to Lucerne. (Detail in [DAY_01.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_01.md))
*   [ ] **Day 2 (Sept 9): Stoos Ridge Hike**
    *   Check weather and webcams before departing Lucerne.
    *   Buy the **"Stoos Peak Experience"** ticket at the Stoosbahn valley station. Present your Swiss Travel Pass to get the STP discount. (Detail in [DAY_02.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_02.md))
*   [ ] **Day 4 (Sept 11): Lauterbrunnen & Trümmelbach Falls**
    *   Store luggage in lockers at Lauterbrunnen station (requires coins/cards).
    *   Buy Trümmelbach Falls tickets on-site (show STP for discount). (Detail in [DAY_04.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_04.md))
*   [ ] **Day 5 (Sept 12): Brienzer Rothorn & Brienz**
    *   Validate ferry schedule at Brienz dock before starting the day.
    *   Bring your pre-booked Brienzer Rothorn tickets and show STP at board/check. (Detail in [DAY_05.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_05.md))
*   [ ] **Day 6 (Sept 13): Grindelwald-First**
    *   Check weather and activity/gondola operating status online before heading to the station.
    *   At the Firstbahn Gondola valley station, purchase the **Adventure Package** (covers 3 activities) + a gondola ticket representing **two ascents** and **one descent**. Present your STP for a 50% discount on the gondola portions. (Detail in [DAY_06.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_06.md))
*   [ ] **Day 7 (Sept 14): Oeschinensee Hike**
    *   Drop luggage at your Interlaken hotel before transit.
    *   At the Kandersteg gondola station, purchase a gondola ticket (present STP for a 50% discount).
    *   Buy tickets for the **Oeschinen Mountain Coaster (Rodelbahn)** on-site at the top station before hiking. (Detail in [DAY_07.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_07.md))
*   [ ] **Day 8 (Sept 15): Harder Kulm & Zermatt Transit**
    *   Buy funicular tickets for Harder Kulm (present STP for discount) at the station or online. (Detail in [DAY_08.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_08.md))
    *   Board the train to Zermatt (fully covered by STP; sit on the **left side** from Visp to Zermatt for the best Matterhorn views).
*   [ ] **Day 9 (Sept 16): Gornergrat & Riffelsee**
    *   Check live webcams at the Gornergrat summit and locate the Valais Blacknose sheep via the online GPS tracker.
    *   Buy Gornergrat Bahn tickets at the station (present STP for a 50% discount). Sit on the **right side** of the train during the ascent. (Detail in [DAY_09.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_09.md))
*   [ ] **Day 10 (Sept 17): Glacier Paradise & Milan Transit**
    *   Buy Matterhorn Glacier Paradise tickets at the station (present STP for discount). (Detail in [DAY_10.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_10.md))
    *   Have passports and pre-booked EuroCity tickets/seat reservations ready for the train journey to Milan.
*   [ ] **Day 11 (Sept 18): Lake Como Day Trip**
    *   Buy regional train tickets from Milano Centrale to Varenna-Esino (sit on the **left side** for lake views).
    *   Purchase mid-lake ferry tickets at Varenna terminal immediately upon arrival to secure your preferred crossing time to Bellagio. (Detail in [DAY_11.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_11.md))
*   [ ] **Day 13 (Sept 20): Self-Guided Milan Exploration**
    *   Pre-purchase self-guided tickets online for Duomo di Milano & Terraces to bypass ticket counter queues. (Detail in [DAY_13.md](file:///c:/Planning/Switzerland2026/DAY_13.md))
    *   Use contactless payment at metro turnstiles for fast transit.
