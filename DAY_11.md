## DAY 11: Lake Como Day Trip

### September 18th, 2026

**Accommodation:** NH Collection Milano CityLife, Milan

**Trip Overview**

Today you will escape the city for the breathtaking beauty of Lake Como. Known for its dramatic alpine scenery, elegant villas, and charming lakeside villages, Lake Como is a quintessential Italian experience. Whether you choose a guided tour or a self-guided adventure by train and ferry, the day will be spent exploring the "Pearl of the Lake," Bellagio, and the picturesque town of Varenna.

---

### **Schedule**

**8:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Breakfast & Transit to Train Station**

*   **Action:** Enjoy breakfast at your hotel.
*   **Transit:** Head to **Milano Centrale** station.

**9:20 AM - 10:23 AM: Train to Varenna-Esino**

*   **The Journey:** Take a direct regional train towards Tirano, getting off at Varenna-Esino.
*   **Tip:** Sit on the **left side** of the train for the best lake views as you approach Varenna.

**10:30 AM - 12:30 PM: Exploring Varenna**

*   **Walk of Lovers:** Stroll along the "Passerella degli Innamorati," the scenic lakeside promenade.
*   **Villa Monastero:** Visit the beautiful gardens of this former monastery (optional).
*   **Village Center:** Explore the narrow, colorful steep streets leading up from the harbor.

**12:30 PM - 1:00 PM: Ferry to Bellagio**

*   **The Crossing:** Take a mid-lake ferry from Varenna to Bellagio (approx. 15-20 minutes). The views from the water are spectacular.

**1:00 PM - 3:30 PM: Lunch & Bellagio Exploration**

*   **Lunch:** Enjoy a lakeside lunch in Bellagio.
*   **The "Pearl of the Lake":** Wander through the world-famous "salita" (steep stone alleys) lined with boutiques and cafes.
*   **Punta Spartivento:** Walk to the very tip of the Bellagio peninsula for a panorama where the three branches of the lake meet.

**3:30 PM - 5:00 PM: Ferry to Como or Back to Varenna**

*   **Option A (Fast):** Take the ferry back to Varenna and catch the train back to Milan.
*   **Option B (Scenic):** Take the "hydrofoil" (fast ferry) all the way down the lake to the city of **Como**, then take a train back to Milan from Como San Giovanni.

**7:00 PM Onwards: Return to Milan & Dinner**

*   **Action:** Arrive back at Milano Centrale or Milano Cadorna.
*   **Dinner:** Enjoy a relaxed dinner back in the CityLife district near your hotel.

---

### **Practical Information & Tips**

*   **Ferry Tickets:** The mid-lake ferry can get busy. Purchase your tickets as soon as you arrive at the ferry terminal.
*   **Guided Tour Alternative:** If you have booked a specific guided tour departing from Milan, please follow your tour provider's meeting instructions. Guided tours often include private boat transfers and visits to Villa del Balbianello.
*   **Footwear:** Expect plenty of walking on cobblestones and steep alleys. Comfortable shoes are essential.
*   **Lake Como Air:** It can be slightly cooler by the lake than in the city; bring a light layer.
