## DAY 12: Milan’s Masterpieces - The Last Supper & Duomo Guided Tours

### September 19th, 2026

**Accommodation:** NH Collection Milano CityLife, Milan

**Trip Overview**

Today is dedicated to two of Italy's greatest artistic and architectural treasures. You will participate in two specialized guided tours: first, a rare and intimate viewing of Leonardo da Vinci's *The Last Supper*, followed by an in-depth exploration of the Duomo di Milano and its magnificent rooftop terraces. These guided experiences will provide rich historical context and skip-the-line access to Milan's most sought-after sites.

---

### **Schedule**

**Morning: Breakfast & Preparation**

*   **Action:** Enjoy breakfast at NH Collection Milano CityLife.
*   **Note:** Ensure you have your tour confirmation details ready (digital or printed).

**10:15 AM - 11:30 AM: Guided Tour of The Last Supper**

*   **Booking Details:** GetYourGuide Reference: `GYG7VKXR3LGN` (1-hour English guided tour organized by Towns of Italy).
*   **The Experience:** Visit the Refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie to see Leonardo da Vinci’s masterpiece. 
*   **Action:** Meet your guide at **10:15 AM** (tour starts at 10:30 AM) in front of the Santa Maria delle Grazie Church (Piazza di Santa Maria delle Grazie, 20123 Milano MI).
*   **Note:** Access is strictly limited to 15-minute time slots; being on time is essential.
*   **Restrictions:** No shorts (shoulders and knees must be covered), no luggage/large bags, and no drinks allowed inside.

**11:30 AM - 2:50 PM: Lunch & Walk to Duomo**

*   **Dining:** Enjoy a leisurely lunch at a bistro in the Magenta district (near Santa Maria delle Grazie).
*   **Transit:** Take a pleasant walk or use the Metro (M1 from Conciliazione or Cadorna to Duomo) to get to the city center.
*   **Action:** Head to the Duomo square for your afternoon tour.

**2:50 PM - 5:00 PM: Milan Duomo & Rooftop Guided Tour**

*   **Booking Details:** GetYourGuide Reference: `GYDKBGA5XQK8` (2-hour English guided tour organized by Wander in Italy).
*   **Action:** Meet your guide at **2:50 PM** (tour starts at 3:00 PM) under the arches of the **Mondadori Megastore**, located on the right-hand side of Piazza del Duomo when facing the front entrance of the cathedral (corner of Via Mazzini). The guide will be holding a sign with the tour name and the "Wander in Italy" logo.
*   **Inside the Duomo:** Explore the vast interior, learning about its 600-year construction history.
*   **The Rooftops:** Ascend to the terraces (via lift) with your guide to walk among the spires and statues.
*   **Restrictions:** Modest dress code applies (no shorts, miniskirts, sleeveless shirts, off-the-shoulder tops, or hats). No weapons, food, drinks, or alcohol allowed. Comfortable shoes are highly recommended.

**5:00 PM - 7:30 PM: Relax & Refresh**

*   **Action:** Return to your hotel or enjoy an espresso/gelato in the elegant Brera district.

**7:30 PM Onwards: Dinner in Milan**

*   **Recommendation:** Consider a restaurant in the **Brera District** for a sophisticated and artistic atmosphere. Try a *Cotoletta alla Milanese* (breaded veal cutlet) or *Risotto alla Milanese* (saffron risotto).

---

### **Practical Information & Tips**

*   **Tour Confirmations:** Check your specific meeting times and locations for both tours. "Skip-the-line" access is included with most guided tours, but security checks are mandatory.
*   **Dress Code:** Both Santa Maria delle Grazie and the Duomo are active religious sites. **Shoulders and knees must be covered.**
*   **Photography:** Photography is generally allowed without flash, but strictly prohibited inside the Refectory of *The Last Supper*.
*   **Walking:** You will be on your feet for several hours during these tours. Wear comfortable walking shoes.
