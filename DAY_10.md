## DAY 10: Matterhorn Glacier Paradise & Journey to Milan

### September 17th, 2026

**Accommodation:** NH Collection Milano CityLife, Milan

**Trip Overview**

On your final day in Zermatt, you will ascend to the highest cable car station in Europe: the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise. After taking in the high-altitude views and exploring the Glacier Palace, you will return to the village for a final lunch. In the afternoon, you will depart the Swiss Alps for the cosmopolitan city of Milan, Italy. The scenic train journey will take you through the Simplon Tunnel and into the heart of Northern Italy.

---

### **Schedule**

**8:30 AM - 9:30 AM: Breakfast & Check-out**

*   **Action:** Enjoy a final breakfast at CERVO. Check out and coordinate your luggage transfer to the Zermatt train station.

**9:30 AM - 12:00 PM: Matterhorn Glacier Paradise**

*   **Ascent:** Take the Matterhorn Express and the "Crystal Ride" cable car to the summit (3,883 m).
*   **Activities:** Visit the 360-degree viewing platform and the Glacier Palace.
*   **Tip:** Allow plenty of time for the descent to ensure you don't feel rushed for your train.

**12:00 PM - 1:30 PM: Final Zermatt Lunch**

*   **Action:** Enjoy a quick but delicious farewell lunch in Zermatt village. Pick up some snacks for the train journey.

**2:00 PM Onwards: Departure for Milan**

*   **Action:** Head to the Zermatt train station for your journey to Milan.
*   **The Journey:**
    *   **Leg 1 (Zermatt -> Visp/Brig):** Take the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn down the valley (approx. 1h 15m - 1h 30m).
    *   **Leg 2 (Brig -> Milano Centrale):** Change to a direct EuroCity (EC) train heading south. This leg takes you through the historic Simplon Tunnel and offers beautiful views as you enter Italy and pass Lake Maggiore (approx. 2h).
*   **Arrival:** Arrive at the magnificent **Milano Centrale** station in the late afternoon or early evening.

**Evening: Check-in & First Night in Milan**

*   **Action:** Take a taxi or the metro to your hotel, **NH Collection Milano CityLife**.
*   **Accommodation:** Check in and settle into your room in this strikingly designed hotel, converted from a former church.
*   **Dinner:** Enjoy your first Italian dinner in the vibrant CityLife district or near the hotel.

---

### **Practical Information & Tips**

*   **Train Booking:** Unlike Swiss domestic trains, the EuroCity train from Brig to Milan **requires a seat reservation**. It is highly recommended to book this in advance.
*   **Swiss Travel Pass:** Your pass covers the journey as far as the Swiss border (usually Domodossola). You will need a separate ticket for the Italian portion of the journey.
*   **Passport:** Have your passport ready, as you are crossing an international border, although checks are often minimal within the Schengen Area.
*   **Milan Transport:** Milan has an excellent metro system. From Milano Centrale, you can reach the CityLife area (Tre Torri or Portello stations) relatively easily.
