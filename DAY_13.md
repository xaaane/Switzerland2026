## DAY 13: Iconic Milan - Duomo, Galleria & Sforza Castle

### September 20th, 2026

**Accommodation:** NH Collection Milano CityLife, Milan

**Trip Overview**

This day is dedicated to exploring the architectural and historic core of Milan at your own pace. You will visit the monumental Duomo, walk through the elegant Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, and explore the sprawling Sforza Castle and its surrounding park. The evening is spent in the lively Navigli district, famous for its canals and traditional Milanese aperitivo.

---

### **Schedule**

**9:00 AM - 10:00 AM: Breakfast & Transit to Centro Storico**

*   **Action:** Enjoy breakfast at your hotel.
*   **Transit:** Take the Metro (Line M5 then M1) to the **Duomo** station.

**10:00 AM - 12:30 PM: Duomo di Milano & Terraces (Self-Guided)**

*   **Action:** Visit the interior of the cathedral and ascend to the rooftop terraces for a final look at the city's skyline and spires.
*   **Tip:** Even if you did a guided tour previously, a self-guided visit allows you to spend more time at specific vantage points for photography.

**12:30 PM - 1:30 PM: Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II**

*   **Action:** Stroll through the historic arcade.
*   **Tradition:** Spin on the bull's mosaic for good luck if you haven't already!

**1:30 PM - 2:30 PM: Lunch in the Center**

*   **Recommendation:** Try a *Panzerotto* from **Luini** or find a trattoria tucked away in the side streets.

**2:30 PM - 4:30 PM: Sforza Castle (Castello Sforzesco) & Parco Sempione**

*   **Action:** Explore the courtyards of the castle and the green expanses of the park. Walk towards the Arco della Pace.

**4:30 PM - 6:30 PM: Afternoon Rest or Shopping**

*   **Action:** Return to CityLife or explore the Quadrilatero della Moda for some world-class window shopping.

**7:00 PM Onwards: Aperitivo & Dinner in Navigli**

*   **Action:** Head to the Navigli area for the classic Milanese aperitivo experience along the canal.

---

### **Practical Information & Tips**

*   **Metro:** Use contactless payment for easy transit.
*   **Planning:** This day is designed to be flexible, allowing you to revisit favorites or explore areas you missed during your guided tours.
